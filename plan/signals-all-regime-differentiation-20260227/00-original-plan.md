# Signals "All" Regime Differentiation

## Goal

Keep the `All` entry in Signals, but make it clearly distinct from Portfolio.

## Problem

`All` currently resembles Portfolio because it starts with rich signal cards and feed-like browsing patterns.

## Direction

- Keep `All` name.
- Position `All` as a **combined regime analysis** surface:
  - aggregate impact summary
  - cross-signal attribution
  - affected holdings concentration
  - combined causal map
- Avoid feed-like signal discovery cards at the top.

## Scope

1. Remove top contribution cards from `All`.
2. Restore compact attribution table (signal-level contribution) in analytical format.
3. Keep and prioritize aggregate sections.
4. Ensure spacing/hierarchy remains Wealthsimple-consistent.
