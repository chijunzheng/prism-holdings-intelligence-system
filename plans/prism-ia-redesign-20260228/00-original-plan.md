# Original Plan: Prism IA Redesign

## Summary
- Introduce a Perplexity-like shell with minimal left rail.
- Keep chat as default home.
- Move holdings and signals to dedicated pages.
- Keep profile lightweight in a modal/sheet.
- Keep plans inside sessions.
- Use progressive disclosure for reasoning trace.
- Remove bulky in-message chips and rely on inline anchors/links.

## Key Decisions
- Routes: `/`, `/holdings`, `/signals`.
- Profile in sheet, not full page.
- Session-embedded plans.
- Action center remains optional and collapsed by default.
